Analysis of Algorithm Efficiency

How to count the operations an algorithm with loops or recursion performs, and how to obtain an exact formula and an efficiency class. Based on A. Levitin's “Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms”, §2.3–2.5.

How to use

Read in order: introduction → Module 1 → Module 2. Each module starts with learning goals, followed by theory and worked examples following the general plan, interactive models, a quiz and problems of three levels. A module ends with a summary and a formula cheat sheet. Terms underlined with a dotted line lead to the glossary; hovering shows a short definition. You can link to any section and any problem: use the # sign next to the heading.
